# Unicorn Warehouse business roadmap

Build one industry at a time. Finish its customer experience and hiring experience before moving to the next business. Once the Portfolio is represented, connect the operations through the backend and seed member history from those operations.

## Checklist for each business

- [ ] Build a front-facing page that a customer would recognize as belonging to that industry. Borrow the familiar interface, merchandising, and visual conventions of the industry.
- [ ] Put the parody in what the interface sells and measures. Prices, add-ons, rankings, quotes, delivery estimates, and pickup screens can carry jokes on their own.
- [ ] Give customers a complete preview of the main action, such as building a meal, checking a delivery quote, or comparing leases. Use stable IDs, prices, and billing units where the offering is defined.
- [ ] Build a Careers page with team art, departments, named managers, and links to the corresponding job postings. Add the team card to the central Careers page.
- [ ] Link the customer page from the homepage directory and the Careers page from the Careers index. Keep existing URLs usable.
- [ ] Put detailed fees, eligibility rules, waivers, and cross-company consequences in Terms or Product Details. Keep the public pitch inviting enough to buy into.
